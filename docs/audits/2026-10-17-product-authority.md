## Three authorities: precise rule

1. **Developing agent:** authorized synthetic/TEST work only; $0 unauthorized spending, no real money or production customer writes, no activating LIVE, no bypassing approved release. These are operational instructions to the implementer, not product ACL.
2. **Product:** expose every feasible Stripe provider capability with actual provider/Stripe-account/account-owner scoping; code gaps are unfinished engineering, not permanent product prohibition.
3. **Actual end user:** trusted independently verified credentials, selected Stripe account and TEST/LIVE mode, native Stripe entitlements, selected profile, pass areas/writes/expiry/rate and user-chosen financial policies. Exact independent authorization for risky effects still applies. Request or tool arguments never invent a pass, enabled account or provider permissions.
