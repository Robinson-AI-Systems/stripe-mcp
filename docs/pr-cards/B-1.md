## B-1b1 — atomic money reservation ledger (PR #19)

- Create additive `money_reservations` table and atomic account-row-locked `reserve_money_out_guarded` PostgreSQL procedure. Enforce Denver local-day grouping and shared per-account daily limit across connections. Never depend on a non-atomic read-then-write sum.
- Preserve idempotency uniqueness across days; mismatched amounts are refused, replay never adds spend, and uncertain/confirmed outcomes continue to count.
- The account daily cap is **USD cents**. Refuse EUR, JPY and other currencies instead of comparing unlike units against a dollar limit. B-1b2 may add verified currency conversion only with a reviewed money-policy amendment; until then fail closed. Do not release capacity automatically on uncertain provider response.
- `src/db.js` and fake DB: `reserveMoneyOut`, `getMoneyUsage`, `settleMoneyReservation`. No tool invokes them until B-1b2.
- `tests/db/money-reservation.test.js`: simultaneous reservation requests on **two independent Postgres clients**; only one may fit the account's limit. `tests/unit/money-reservation.test.js`: fake contract.
- No real Stripe calls or money actions. CI exact-head green before merge.

## B-1b2 — guarded monetary writes, tests and approval gate (PR #20)

- Implement `src/money.js` integer USD formatter and atomic ledger hooks. The owner-approved $500 cap is enforced per Stripe account across connections. No `moneyPolicyApproved` flag is trusted; transaction-specific database owner approval is required for every money-out and a separately recorded owner override is required over the daily cap. The money spec derives cents from the authoritative provider state, never the caller's `amountCents`.
- Enforce across concurrent serverless calls with a database-backed atomic mechanism; an ordinary read-then-write sum is **not sufficient** to prevent two simultaneous over-limit operations.
- Complete `tests/helpers/write-contract.js` and `tests/unit/write-contract.test.js`: seven design cases on sample money and non-money tools, including app-owned guard callback, live deny, default money-deny, USD dollar line and daily cap. Real independent-PostgreSQL concurrency tests are in #19.
- The internal safety primitives can merge without activation. The owner approval browser flow remains an explicit later gate: no AI-accessible write is enabled by B-1. No profile/live/app guard is widened.
- Never widen live/profile access or expose any write tool before B-2/B-3/B-6 guards.
