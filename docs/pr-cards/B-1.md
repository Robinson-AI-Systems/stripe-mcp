## B-1b1 — atomic money reservation ledger (PR #19)

- Create additive `money_reservations` table and atomic account-row-locked `reserve_money_out_guarded` PostgreSQL procedure. Enforce Denver local-day grouping and per-account-and-connection daily limit. Never depend on a non-atomic read-then-write sum.
- Preserve idempotency uniqueness across days; mismatched amounts are refused, replay never adds spend, and uncertain/confirmed outcomes continue to count.
- The account daily cap is **USD cents**. Refuse EUR, JPY and other currencies instead of comparing unlike units against a dollar limit. B-1b2 may add verified currency conversion only with a reviewed money-policy amendment; until then fail closed. Do not release capacity automatically on uncertain provider response.
- `src/db.js` and fake DB: `reserveMoneyOut`, `getMoneyUsage`, `settleMoneyReservation`. No tool invokes them until B-1b2.
- `tests/db/money-reservation.test.js`: simultaneous reservation requests on **two independent Postgres clients**; only one may fit the account's limit. `tests/unit/money-reservation.test.js`: fake contract.
- No real Stripe calls or money actions. CI exact-head green before merge.
